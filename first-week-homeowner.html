Your First Week as a Homeowner

The essential day-by-day guide to settling into your new DFW home. Complete these priorities to set yourself up for success.

Last updated: February 2026

Day 1: Safety First

Before you unpack a single box, ensure your home is safe and secure. These tasks could save your life.

🔥 Fire Safety

Test all smoke detectors
Press the test button on every unit. Replace batteries if needed. Detectors should be on every level and outside bedrooms.
Test carbon monoxide detectors
CO is odorless and deadly. Test all units, especially near bedrooms and furnace areas.
Locate or buy a fire extinguisher
Check if previous owner left one. If not, buy a multi-purpose ABC extinguisher for the kitchen.

💧 Water & Utilities

Find main water shutoff valve
Usually near water meter or where main line enters house. Test it works. Tag it clearly.
Locate gas shutoff valve
Near gas meter. You'll need a wrench. Keep one nearby. Only shut off in emergencies.
Find electrical panel and test breakers
Verify all breakers are labeled. Test main breaker. Take photo of panel layout.
DFW TIP

Texas Weather Hazards

  • Locate your tornado shelter area (interior room, lowest floor, no windows)
  • Know where outdoor water faucets are for freeze protection
  • Check attic ventilation in hot months - DFW heat is brutal
  • Subscribe to local weather alerts (NWS Fort Worth, local news apps)

Day 2: Secure Your Home

Protect your investment and your family. These security measures should be in place before your first night.

🔐 Locks & Access

Change or rekey all locks
You don't know who has copies of old keys. Hire a locksmith or DIY with rekeying kits.
Reset garage door code
Reprogram keypads and remotes. Check manual or look up your model online.
Check all window locks
Test every window latch. Replace broken locks. Consider window bars for ground-level windows.
Install deadbolts on exterior doors
If not already installed, add Grade 1 deadbolts to all exterior doors. Simple DIY project.

📹 Security Systems

Transfer or install security system
Contact existing provider or research new systems (Ring, SimpliSafe, ADT).
Consider a video doorbell
See who's at your door from anywhere. Popular options: Ring, Nest, Arlo.
DFW TIP

Local Security Considerations

  • Join your neighborhood's NextDoor or Facebook group for local crime alerts
  • Register with your city's police department for vacation watch services
  • Package theft is common - consider lockboxes or porch lockers
  • Many DFW neighborhoods have active crime watch programs

DFW TIP

Texas-Specific Systems

  • Most DFW homes have two HVAC systems (upstairs/downstairs) - find both
  • Check if you're on city water or well system
  • Locate outdoor faucet shutoff valves for freeze protection
  • If you have a pool, find the pump, filter, and heater controls

📸 Photo Documentation

Photograph every room
Document move-in condition for insurance and future reference. Include walls, floors, ceilings.
Photo all system serial numbers
HVAC, water heater, appliances. Useful for warranty claims and service calls.
Record utility meter readings
Photo water, gas, and electric meters with readings. Helps resolve billing disputes.

Day 5: Get Organized

Set up systems that will save you time, money, and stress for years to come.

📞 Emergency Contacts

Fill in these numbers and post on your fridge. You'll need them eventually.

🗂️ Organization Tasks

Set up maintenance calendar
Use phone reminders for HVAC filters (monthly), detector batteries (yearly), etc.
Assemble basic tool kit
Hammer, screwdrivers, pliers, wrench, tape measure, flashlight, duct tape.
Stock emergency supplies
Flashlights, batteries, water shutoff tool, fire ladder (if 2+ stories).
DFW TIP

Local Service Providers

  • Ask neighbors for trusted contractor recommendations on NextDoor
  • Many DFW cities have preferred vendor lists on their websites
  • For HVAC, look for companies with 24/7 emergency service (critical in DFW summer)
  • Keep numbers for tree trimming services - ice storms and tornadoes happen

Day 6: Connect With Your Community

Meet your neighbors and establish yourself in the community. Good relationships matter.

👋 Neighbor Connections

Introduce yourself to immediate neighbors
At minimum, meet the neighbors on both sides and across the street. Exchange phone numbers.
Join neighborhood social media groups
NextDoor, Facebook groups, or community apps. Great for recommendations and alerts.

📍 Local Services

Learn trash and recycling schedule
Check city website or ask neighbors. Set reminders so you don't miss pickup.
Confirm mail forwarding
Verify USPS forwarding is active. Meet your mail carrier if possible.
Locate nearby essentials
Find closest grocery store, pharmacy, gas station, hospital, and bank.
DFW TIP

Getting Involved Locally

  • Many neighborhoods have annual HOA meetings, block parties, or National Night Out events
  • Sign up for city newsletters to stay informed about local issues
  • Check if your area has a community pool or rec center with resident rates
  • Look into local farmers markets, food trucks, and community events

Day 7: Celebrate and Reflect

Take a moment to appreciate what you've accomplished. You're now officially a homeowner.

🎉 Celebrate Your Achievement

Give yourself a full house tour
Walk through as the owner, not the buyer. Notice what you love about your home.
Cook your first meal at home
Even if it's just toast. Start creating memories in your new space.
Take photos of your first week
Document this milestone. You'll love looking back years from now.

📝 Plan Your Next Steps

Create your improvement wish list
Write down everything you want to change or upgrade. Prioritize by urgency and budget.
Review your homeownership budget
Now that you've moved in, adjust budget for actual utilities, maintenance costs, etc.
Schedule first maintenance appointments
Book HVAC tune-up, gutter cleaning, or other seasonal maintenance now.
DFW TIP

Looking Ahead

  • Set aside 1-3% of home value annually for maintenance and repairs
  • Before DFW summer hits, schedule AC maintenance - companies book up fast
  • Consider a home warranty for the first year while you learn your home
  • Join local homeowner groups for DIY tips and contractor recommendations

What NOT to Do as a New Homeowner

Avoid these common mistakes that cost first-time homeowners thousands in the first year.

⚠️

7 Costly Mistakes to Avoid

Learn from others' expensive lessons

1
Skipping Home Inspections

Never ignore recommended follow-up inspections. That $500 specialist inspection could save you $10,000 in repairs.

2
Neglecting HVAC Maintenance

Change filters monthly, schedule annual tune-ups. A $2,000 AC repair in July is preventable.

3
Over-Improving Too Soon

Live in your home 6-12 months before major renovations. You'll make smarter choices.

4
Ignoring Small Leaks

Water damage compounds fast. Fix leaks immediately. Mold remediation costs $3,000-$10,000+.

5
Not Reading HOA Rules

Violations can result in fines. Know the rules before painting, landscaping, or adding structures.

6
Hiring the Cheapest Contractor

Low bids often mean cut corners. Check licenses, insurance, and references. Always.

7
Forgetting to Winterize (Yes, in Texas)

DFW gets hard freezes. Cover outdoor faucets, insulate pipes. Remember Winter Storm Uri.

Key Takeaways

  • Safety first: Test all detectors, locate shutoff valves, and know your emergency exits before unpacking
  • Security matters: Change locks, reset codes, and install basic security measures within 48 hours
  • Document everything: Photos, receipts, and manuals will save you thousands when issues arise
  • Know your systems: Locate and understand every major system before you need to troubleshoot
  • Build relationships: Good neighbors and local contractors are invaluable resources
  • Avoid rush decisions: Live in your home before making major changes or improvements
  • Maintenance prevents disasters: Small investments in upkeep prevent massive repair bills
