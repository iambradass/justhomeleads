    The questions you're a little embarrassed to ask.

    Everyone has them. Here are the real answers, no textbook required.

    • Do I really need 20% down? No. Plenty of loans start at 3% to 3.5%, and Texas has down payment help most people never hear about.
    • My credit is bad. Is it over? Not even close. Some loans work with scores in the 500s, and a few fixes can move your score in 60 to 90 days.
    • What else do I pay besides the down payment? Closing costs, usually 2% to 5% of the price. Here's every line item, and which ones the seller can cover.
    • Should I wait for rates to drop? Maybe. Here's how to tell if waiting saves you money or just costs you another year of rent.
    • Do I even need an agent? Short answer: yes, but not just any agent. Don't hire your cousin unless she's a shark.
    • Why are Texas property taxes so high? No state income tax, so homes carry the load. Here's what you'll really pay and how the homestead exemption cuts it.
