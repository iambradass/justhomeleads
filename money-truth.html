Down Payment

Your largest upfront cost, but smaller than you think

FHA Down Payment (3.5%)
$13,300
Best for

First-time buyers with credit scores 580-700 who want to get into a home faster. You'll pay mortgage insurance (MIP) for the life of the loan, but you're building equity instead of paying rent.

Conventional Down Payment (5%)
$19,000
Best for

Buyers with 680+ credit who want slightly better rates than FHA. PMI can be removed once you reach 20% equity (unlike FHA's permanent MIP).

Conventional Down Payment (10%)
$38,000
Best for

Buyers who've saved more and want lower monthly payments and lower PMI costs. Good middle ground.

Conventional Down Payment (20%)
$76,000
Best for

Buyers who want lowest monthly payment and no PMI. But ask yourself: is it worth waiting 2-3 more years to save this? You'd pay $36,000+ in rent while saving.

Pro Tip

You can negotiate! Ask the seller to pay part of your closing costs (called "seller concessions"). In a buyer's market, sellers often cover 2-3% of closing costs. Even in competitive markets, you might get 1-2%.

The P&I Trap

When you search Zillow, they show "Est. payment: $2,440." That's just P&I! Your ACTUAL payment with taxes, insurance, and PMI is $3,603+. Always calculate the FULL payment before falling in love with a house.

Hidden & First-Year Costs

The stuff that catches new homeowners completely off guard

Budget for These (Most People Don't)

Moving Costs

$1,500-$4,000

Professional movers, truck rental, boxes, supplies. More if coming from apartment with stairs.

Immediate Repairs

$1,000-$3,000

Things you discover after moving in. Leaky faucet, broken sprinkler head, garage door issues.

Lawn Equipment

$300-$800

Mower, trimmer, hoses, sprinkler, tools. Or budget $100-150/month for lawn service.

Window Coverings

$500-$2,000

Blinds, curtains, shades. Most homes come with nothing on windows.

Appliances

$0-$3,000

Refrigerator often not included. May need washer/dryer.

Furniture

$1,000-$5,000+

Bigger rooms need bigger furniture. Outdoor furniture. Extra bedroom furniture.

Utility Deposits

$200-$500

Electric, gas, water, internet setup fees and deposits.

Annual Maintenance

$3,800/year

Budget 1% of home value annually. HVAC service, filters, pest control, small repairs.

Don't Forget Reserves

Most lenders want to see 2-3 months of mortgage payments in reserves AFTER closing. On a $3,600/month payment, that's $7,200-$10,800 you can't touch. Budget for this on top of your closing funds.

Key Takeaways

  • Buying a $380K home in DFW requires $25,000-$42,000 in cash depending on your down payment and closing cost negotiations
  • Your monthly payment will be $3,600-$4,000 including taxes, insurance, and PMI (NOT just the P&I shown on Zillow)
  • Plan for another $8,000-$20,000 in first-year costs: moving, repairs, furniture, maintenance
  • You can negotiate seller concessions to cover 1-3% of closing costs in many markets
  • If you stay 5+ years and can afford it comfortably, you'll build wealth instead of paying rent
